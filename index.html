Ask ViVi
How long does permanent makeup last?

Usually 1–3 years, depending on your skin type, lifestyle and aftercare. A touch-up every 12–18 months keeps the color fresh.

Does it hurt?

We use a professional topical numbing cream, so most clients only feel a light scratching sensation.

What is the difference between stroke brows and ombré brows?

Stroke brows create individual hair-like lines and suit dry to normal skin. Ombré brows give a soft powdered effect and suit all skin types, including oily skin.

How should I prepare, and what is the aftercare?

Avoid alcohol and caffeine for 24 hours before. Afterwards, keep the area dry for 7 days, skip makeup on it and avoid sun exposure.

Can you correct work done elsewhere?

Yes. ViVi will assess your current brows or lips and recommend the right option — lightening, color correction or a cover-up.
